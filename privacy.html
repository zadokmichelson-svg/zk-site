PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: November 4, 2026

ZK Detailers ("we," "us," or "our") is a mobile auto detailing business on the Big Island of Hawaiʻi. This policy works together with our Terms of Service and explains what information we collect when you visit zkdetailers.com, request a quote, or book a detail, and how we use and protect it. We do not sell your personal information.

1.Information We Collect

We only collect what we need to quote, schedule, and complete your detail:

  • Contact info: your name, phone number, and email address
  • Service address: where you'd like us to perform the detail
  • Vehicle info: make, model, vehicle type, and condition
  • Booking details: the package, add-ons, and appointment time you choose
  • Photos: pictures of your vehicle that you text or send us for a quote
  • Messages: texts, calls, emails, and social media messages you send us
  • Payment info: handled by our payment processor (see Section 4). We do not store your full card number.

2.How We Use Your Information

  • To give you a quote and confirm your appointment
  • To find your location and arrive on time
  • To contact you about your booking, including reminders, delays, or changes
  • To process payments and keep basic business and tax records
  • To follow up after your detail and ask for feedback or a review
  • To send occasional offers, such as maintenance plan or seasonal deals, only if you've agreed to receive them. You can opt out anytime.

3.Photos of Your Vehicle

We may take before-and-after photos of vehicles we detail for our records. We will not share identifiable photos of your vehicle on our website or social media (such as Instagram and Facebook) without your permission. If you give permission:

  • We blur or crop out license plates and other identifying details
  • We do not show people, faces, home addresses, or personal items inside the vehicle
  • You can change your mind at any time, and we will take the photos down

4.Who We Share Information With

We only share your information with services we use to run the business, and only as needed:

  • Payment processing: such as Square, which handles card payments securely
  • Scheduling and communication tools: such as calendar, email, and text messaging apps
  • Team members: anyone who helps perform your detail may see your name, address, and vehicle info
  • Legal reasons: if required by law or to protect our business, customers, or others

We never sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

5.Website, Cookies & Advertising

Our website may use tools such as Google Analytics, the Meta (Facebook) Pixel, or similar services to understand how visitors use the site and to measure our ads. These tools may use cookies to collect information like your browser type, pages visited, and general location. They do not give us your name or contact info.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and you can manage ad preferences in your Facebook, Instagram, and Google account settings.

If you message us through Facebook or Instagram, or submit a lead form on those platforms, Meta's privacy policy also applies to that information.

6.How We Protect Your Information

We keep your information on secure, password-protected devices and accounts, and only for as long as we need it for business, tax, or legal reasons. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you share with us.

7.Your Choices

  • See or update your info: ask us what we have and we'll correct anything that's wrong
  • Delete your info: ask us to delete it, and we will, except for records we're required to keep (like payment and tax records)
  • Stop messages: reply STOP to texts or tell us you no longer want offers
  • Remove photos: withdraw your photo permission and we'll take down any photo of your vehicle

8.Children

Our services are for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

9.Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The newest version will always be on this page with its effective date.
