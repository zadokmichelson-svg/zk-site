1.Booking Requests and Confirmation

Submitting a request through our website, quote calculator, text message, phone call, email, or social media is a booking request, not a guaranteed appointment. An appointment is confirmed only after ZK Detailers accepts the request and confirms the service date, time, location, package, add-ons, and estimated price with you.

2.Services, Pricing, and Estimates

Package descriptions and posted prices describe the standard scope of each service. All prices shown on our website and quote calculator are estimates based on the information you provide.

  • Package pricing is based on vehicle type (Small Car, Sedan, SUV/Truck, or Van/Work Van). Vehicles that are oversized, modified, or do not fit a listed category may be quoted individually.
  • Add-ons listed "per seat" are charged for each seat serviced. Add-ons listed as a price range are priced within that range based on vehicle size and condition.
  • Services included in your selected package will not also be charged as add-ons.

Final pricing may change based on the vehicle's actual size, condition, or the services required. We will tell you about any price change before performing the additional work, and you may decline it.

4.Monthly Maintenance Plan

Customers who purchase a Premium detail may enroll in our Monthly Maintenance Plan.

  • What's included: one Basic maintenance detail per month, as described in our Basic package.
  • Price: $150 per month, plus any applicable taxes and Additional Fees (Section 3).
  • Scheduling: maintenance visits are scheduled in advance by mutual agreement. A visit that is not scheduled or completed within its month does not carry over to the next month, unless the delay was caused by ZK Detailers or weather.
  • Add-ons: add-ons may be added to any maintenance visit at their posted prices.
  • Cancellation: the plan is month-to-month. You may cancel at any time by notifying us before your next scheduled visit.
  • Plan changes: we may change plan pricing or terms for future months with at least 30 days' notice.

5.Cancellations, No-Shows, and Rescheduling

We reserve time and travel for every appointment, so we ask for advance notice if your plans change.

  • 24 hours or more notice: you may cancel or reschedule at no charge.
  • Less than 24 hours notice: a late cancellation fee of $25–$50 may apply.
  • No-show: if we arrive at the confirmed time and location and cannot access the vehicle or reach you within 15 minutes, the appointment may be treated as a no-show and the fee may apply.

No cancellation fee applies when we cancel or reschedule, including for weather or safety reasons (Section 9). The cancellation fee may be waived at our discretion for emergencies.

6.Customer Responsibilities

To help us deliver safe, quality service, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate vehicle, contact, service location, and condition information.
  • Ensure we have lawful and safe access to the vehicle, and that you own the vehicle or have the owner's permission for the requested work.
  • Provide a suitable work area and, when requested, access to water and electrical power.
  • Remove cash, valuables, sensitive documents, child car seats, and other important personal property before service.
  • Disclose known damage, loose trim, electrical issues, leaks, delicate modifications, recent paint or body work, and any other conditions that may affect safe detailing.
  • Be reasonably available during the appointment for questions and payment, and be present (or have someone present for you) for the price confirmation before work begins and the final walkthrough when it is finished.

9.Weather, Safety, and Right to Refuse Service

Mobile service depends on suitable weather, lighting, access, and safe working conditions. We may pause, relocate, reschedule, or decline service when conditions could endanger people, property, or equipment, or prevent a quality result. This includes heavy rain, high winds, unsafe locations, and vehicles containing hazardous materials, pests, or mold beyond the scope of our services.

If we reschedule for these reasons, no fee will be charged, and we will work with you to find the next available time.
